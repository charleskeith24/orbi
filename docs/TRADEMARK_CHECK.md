# Orbi — trademark pre-check

> **Not legal advice.** This is desk research from public web pages, done on 14 September 2026 to
> help decide whether "Orbi" is safe to build a paid product on. It is **not** a trademark search
> report or a legal opinion. We could not search the official registries directly (see
> [What we could not verify](#what-we-could-not-verify)). Before you sell Orbi, file a trademark, or
> spend real money on the name, get an official search and talk to a Philippine IP lawyer or
> IPOPHL-accredited trademark agent.

---

## The short answer

**Risk for "Orbi" as the name of a paid creator/marketing app: high.**

1. **Another "Orbi" already sells almost the same product.** Orbi at orbiai.app is "an AI-powered
   content creation platform" for solo creators, teams and agencies. It covers niche trend
   monitoring, ideas and drafts from your "Brand DNA", pre-publish scoring of hooks, scheduling to
   Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, and performance analytics. Its footer says © 2026 Oona Flairlab
   GmbH, a company from Düsseldorf, Germany. [1][2] Same name, same buyers, same features: this is
   the conflict most likely to cause real confusion. We don't know whether they filed a trademark
   (see below).
2. **NETGEAR's ORBI® is famous in tech, including in the Philippines.** NETGEAR lists "ORBI®" among
   its current trademarks. [3] It sells Orbi mesh Wi-Fi systems plus an **Orbi app** for iOS and
   Android. [4] Orbi units are sold by Philippine retailers [5], and NETGEAR has a Philippine page
   with a Manila address. [6] The app puts NETGEAR in the same class as downloadable software
   (class 9). Philippine law also protects well-known marks registered in the Philippines against
   use on unrelated goods, where the use would suggest a connection with the owner and hurt its
   interests (IP Code §123.1(f)). [7][8] We could not confirm whether ORBI is registered in the
   Philippines or has been declared well-known there.
3. **"Orbi" is crowded in software generally.** Other examples:
   - A Swedish student app, run by Ornamental Tree AB in Gothenburg, that sells a "Marketing
     Suite" and an "HR Suite" to companies. [9][10][11]
   - An "Orbi" listed on Capterra as a recruitment-marketing platform. [12]
   - A voice-based social app called Orbi. [13]
   - Several US records with ORBI in the mark (see the table).

**Bottom line:** "Orbi" is fine as a working name for the beta, but it is a weak foundation for a
product you want to sell and defend. Run the official searches below for "Orbi" and 2–3 of the
alternatives, and get a lawyer's opinion before launch. The orbit logo can stay with almost any of
the alternatives.

## Which trademark classes matter for Orbi

Trademarks are registered for specific goods and services, grouped into the 45 Nice classes. The
Philippines uses the Nice Classification. [14] For Orbi:

| Class | Covers (simplified) | Why it matters for Orbi |
|---|---|---|
| **9** | Apparatus and IT equipment, including **downloadable software and apps** [15] | The installable Orbi app (PWA / phone app) |
| **42** | Computer and technology services, including **software as a service (SaaS)** [15][16] | Orbi as an online service creators log into |
| **35** | Advertising, marketing and business-management services [15] | If you sell content strategy, marketing or social-media management *services* |

Most software products file in 9 and 42; add 35 if you offer marketing services. [16] Also watch
neighbouring classes: 38 (telecommunications, where NETGEAR operates) and 41 (education and
entertainment, e.g. creator courses).

## How to search yourself

Plan about an hour. Search **"ORBI"** first, then sound-alikes and variants: ORBY, ORBEE, ORBII,
ORBEY, ORBIT, ORBI AI. Then search each alternative name you like. For every hit, write down:
mark, owner, application or registration number, filing date, **status** (pending, registered,
expired, refused, abandoned), classes, the goods/services text, and whether there's a logo. Take
dated screenshots.

### 1. IPOPHL (Philippines) — the one that matters most

IPOPHL's trademark database holds pending, registered and expired Philippine marks. It launched in
2013 with nearly 300,000 records and was modelled on WIPO's Global Brand Database. [31] Philippine
trademark data is also in TMview (since 23 March 2015). [32]

1. Go to IPOPHL's **Trademark Online Tools** page:
   <https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/trademark/trademark-online-tools/>. Open the trademark search
   database listed there. Tool names change over time; you may see "eSearch" or "TMview". The
   e-Services portal is at <https://onlineservices.ipophil.gov.ph/>. [33]
2. Search by **mark text** ("orbi"). The database also searches by **applicant/holder name** and
   **application number**. [34]
3. Repeat with the variants above. Then search the holder **"NETGEAR"** to see all of its
   Philippine marks, and **"Oona Flairlab"**.
4. In the results, look at classes **9, 35 and 42** first, then 38 and 41.
5. Open each close hit and note its status and goods/services text.
6. IPOPHL's TMview can produce a preliminary search report you can keep for your records. [32]

### 2. WIPO Global Brand Database (international and Madrid filings)

1. Open <https://branddb.wipo.int/>.
2. Type **ORBI** in the brand-name field. Start with exact matching, then widen to fuzzy or
   phonetic matching if the tool offers it.
3. Filter **Designation = PH** (international registrations that cover the Philippines). Then
   filter **Office = PH** (the Philippine national collection, which is in the database). [35]
   Then search again with no country filter for the global picture.
4. Filter **Nice class** 9, 35 and 42. Other filters include status and dates. [36]
5. Try the **image search** with the Orbi logo to find similar orbit-ring logos. [36]
6. Open each record for its status, owner, dates and goods/services.

### 3. USPTO (United States) — if you'll sell to US creators or ship to the US app stores

1. Open USPTO **Trademark Search**: <https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/>. It replaced the old TESS system
   on 30 November 2023. [37]
2. Basic search: choose **Wordmark**, type `orbi`, and include dead marks. Advanced/expert mode
   lets you search `orbi*` limited to classes 009, 035 and 042. [38]
3. Look up the serial numbers in the table above (86788272, 85517210, 98103334, 98951084,
   79319485). Open each one's **TSDR** record for its real status.

### 4. Also check

- **Business names:** SEC (corporations) and DTI (sole proprietorships) name searches. [39]
- **App stores:** search "Orbi" in the Philippine Play Store and App Store.
- **Domains and handles:** orbi.ph, orbi.app, and @orbi on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

## Next steps

1. **Don't spend on the name yet.** Hold off on domains, paid ads, printed merch and a logo refresh
   until step 3. The beta can keep "Orbi" as a working name. Don't use the ® symbol; it is only
   for registered marks.
2. **Shortlist 2–3 names** from the list below and run the IPOPHL and WIPO searches for each one
   and for "Orbi".
3. **Book a Philippine IP lawyer or IPOPHL-accredited agent** for a clearance opinion on classes 9,
   35 and 42. Bring your search notes. Ask about:
   - the risk from NETGEAR's ORBI (famous mark);
   - the risk from Orbi at orbiai.app (same market);
   - whether to file the word mark and the orbit logo separately;
   - whether to file in the Philippines first and extend abroad later through the Madrid System
     (IPOPHL supports it). [40]
4. **File with IPOPHL** (online through e-Services) for the chosen name. After filing, IPOPHL
   examines the application, publishes it in the E-Gazette for opposition, and then registers
   it. [39]
5. **Put the Declaration of Actual Use (DAU) in your calendar.** It must be filed within **3 years
   of the filing date**, or the application is refused or the mark removed. [39] Keep dated proof
   of use: screenshots, invoices, launch posts.

## Alternative names that keep the orbit logo

These are chosen so the orbit ring still makes sense. Filipino words about turning or going around
also tell the product's story: strategy → create → publish → measure → **repeat**. The conflict
notes come from quick web searches, **not registry searches**. Run each finalist through IPOPHL.

| # | Name | Meaning / why it fits | Quick conflict note (web search) | First take |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Inog** ("ee-NOG") | Tagalog: rotation, revolution; turning on an axis or around a centre [41][42] | Nearest: Inogic (Microsoft Dynamics add-ons; different spelling), Inog Solutions (industrial control software), an "INOG" clinic app in Spain [43]. Nothing in creator or social tools. | **Promising.** Short, Filipino, the orbit idea itself. |
| 2 | **Buntala** | Filipino coinage for planet or comet (from *buntot* + *tala*) [44] | No product found. The closest spelling, "Buntata", is a UK plant-disease app [45]. | **Promising.** Distinctive; the meaning is a nice brand story. |
| 3 | **Ligid** | Tagalog: around, surroundings [46] | No app or brand found. Also part of a barangay name (Ligid-Tipas, Taguig) [46]. | **Promising.** Ask the lawyer whether the place name matters. |
| 4 | **Libot** | Tagalog: to go around, to wander [47] | A small hobby site "LiBot" and a barangay page [47]. | **Promising**, though it leans towards travel or touring. |
| 5 | **Palibot** | Tagalog: surroundings, all around [48] | No app or brand found [48]. | OK. Longer; "…bot" may read as a chatbot. |
| 6 | **Ikot** | Tagalog: turn, go around, loop | **ikotMNL**, the LRT-1 railway and tourism app by Light Rail Manila Corp; also Eco-Ikot, a waste app [49]. | **Medium risk.** Different field, but the same country and app stores. |
| 7 | **Orrery** | A clockwork model of the solar system: a literal orbit logo | Used by several small software makers (a London studio, astronomy apps) [50]. | Medium. Hard to spell and say for many creators. |
| 8 | **Orbita** | Spanish/Filipino for "orbit" | Orbita (healthcare AI); **Orbita for GoLogin**, a browser for affiliate, social-media and e-commerce profiles; Órbita, a digital-marketing course [51]. | **High risk.** Marketing overlap, and very close to "Orbi". |
| 9 | **Perigee** | An orbit's closest point | Perigee Software (integrations), Perigee AB (fitness apps), Perigee Labs (app development) [52]. | Crowded. |
| 10 | **Orbitly** / **Apsis** | Orbit-flavoured coinages | Orbitly: a lead-enrichment platform, a day-planner app and more [53]. APSIS One: a Swedish marketing-automation platform with 3,600+ business customers [54]. | **Avoid.** |

**Suggested shortlist to search officially:** Inog, Buntala, Ligid.
